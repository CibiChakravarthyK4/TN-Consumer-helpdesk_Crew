TAMIL NADU CONSUMER HELPDESK & UTILITY GUIDELINES (20+ REFERENCE POINTS)

1. TNEB (Tamil Nadu Electricity Board / TANGEDCO) Services:
1. Power Outages & Emergencies: Dial toll-free helpline 1912, operational 24x7 for fuse-off calls and power cuts.
2. WhatsApp Support: Send photos of damaged poles, low-hanging wires, or hazards to TNEB WhatsApp line 9498794987.
3. Online Bill Payments & History: Access tnebnet.org to download receipts, check consumption patterns, and pay bills securely.
4. Name Transfer (Mutation): Service connection name transfers can be initiated online by submitting property tax receipts and sale deeds.
5. New Service Connection: Applications for domestic, commercial, or agricultural connections are processed via the official TANGEDCO portal within statutory timeframes.
6. Voltage Fluctuation Complaints: Low or high voltage issues can be logged via 1912 or the local section office (AE/JE).
7. Smart Metering Queries: Inquiries regarding smart prepaid/postpaid meter installations and recharge tracking.
8. Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF): Unresolved complaints at the circle level can be escalated to the CGRF.
9. Electricity Ombudsman: Final appellate authority if consumers are unsatisfied with CGRF decisions.
10. Theft or Misuse Reporting: Energy theft and illegal tapping can be anonymously reported to the TNEB Enforcement and Flying Squad wings.

II. Public Distribution System (PDS) & Smart Ration Cards:
11. State Consumer Helpline: Contact 044-28592828 for civil supplies, ration card queries, and general consumer grievances.
12. New Smart Ration Card: Applications for new family cards, additions, or deletions of members are processed through the TN PDS portal.
13. Fair Price Shop Issues: Complaints regarding short weighting, adulteration, or irregular shop timings can be registered directly with Taluk Supply Officers.
14. Commodity Entitlements: Details on monthly subsidized rice, sugar, kerosene, and pulses distributed via ration shops.
15. Address Change Requests: Procedure for updating residential addresses and changing head of family details on smart cards.

III. Public Transport & Metro Services:
16. Chennai Metro Rail (CMRL): Smart card purchases, online top-ups, and QR ticketing assistance.
17. MTC Bus Pass Services: Application guidelines and renewal processes for student, senior citizen, and general commuter bus passes.
18. Transport Department / RTO: Driving license renewals, vehicle registration transfers, and fitness certificate queries via the Parivahan / TN transport portals.
19. Fare & Ticketing Disputes: Refund processing timelines (typically 3-5 business days) for failed digital transit transactions.
20. Public Transport Grievances: Registering complaints regarding crew behavior, route diversions, or unhygienic terminal facilities.

IV. General Consumer Protection & Rights:
21. Consumer Protection Act: Guidelines on filing cases against unfair trade practices, defective products, or deficient services.
22. E-Daakhil Portal: Filing consumer court cases online for disputes up to district and state commission limits.
23. District Consumer Forums: Mediation and redressal mechanisms for local marketplace grievances and warranty frauds.
